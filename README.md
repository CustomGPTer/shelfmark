# Shelfmark

Shelfmark is a free Android ebook reader that finds your books, shelves each one under a proper bookshop heading, and syncs your place through your own Google Drive. No adverts, nothing to unlock.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/shelfmark/

Download: https://github.com/CustomGPTer/shelfmark/releases/latest/download/Shelfmark.apk
