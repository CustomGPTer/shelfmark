Free in Shelfmark, paid elsewhere

Shelfmark has no paid version, no subscription and no adverts. Several things that other Android ebook readers sell as an upgrade are simply part of it.

Download for Android

Free. Android 10 or later. What it does

Readers tied to a bookshop

Kobo Books, Kindle and Google Play Books are free and have no paid edition. Each is built around the books its own shop sells. This is how each one handles ebook files you already have.
