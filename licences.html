Books that come with the app

Shelfmark comes with four novels so there is something to read straight away: Pride and Prejudice by Jane Austen, The Picture of Dorian Gray by Oscar Wilde, The Time Machine by H. G. Wells and A Study in Scarlet by Arthur Conan Doyle. All four texts are in the public domain.
