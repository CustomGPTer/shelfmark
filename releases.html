Release notes

What changed in each version of Shelfmark. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.22 14 November 2026Latest

  • Reading stats and a tidier Authors list. Reading stats. Tap the goal card on Home, or choose More, then Reading stats, to see time read, books finished, pages read and days read for the week, month, year or all time, each compared with the one before.
  • Charts of your reading: time for each day or month with your daily goal marked, the days of the week and times of day you read, and a square for every day of the last six months.
  • The books and authors you spent longest with, what you finished by kind and genre, and the non-fiction subjects you covered, with your highlights, notes and authors new to you.
  • A goal for the number of books to finish in a year, showing whether you are ahead or behind for the date, and milestones with the next one to aim for.
  • A book's details show how long you have spent reading it.
  • The Authors list is in surname order, with letters down the right-hand edge to jump by.
  • Different spellings of one author's name are shown as one author, and a book by two authors is listed under each of them.
  • Books whose author box holds something that is not a name are gathered under Unknown author at the end of the Authors list. Nothing stored on the books is changed.
  • The goal card on Home can be hidden from the reading stats as well as from Settings.
  • With sync on, the new reading figures are kept in step across your devices.
  • The On phone and In Drive buttons no longer appear for a moment when the Library opens with no Drive books in it.

Version 1.21

  • A fuller Series list. Each series shows its author underneath and how many of its books you have read.
  • Letters down the right-hand edge of the Series list jump to that part of the list. Tap one or slide a finger along them.
  • Next in series on Home: when you finish a book in a series, the next one you own is waiting there.
  • Inside a series, a line at the top names the numbers missing from your set.
  • The Series list is in bookshop order, so a leading A, An or The is ignored.
  • Coming back from a series, an author or a category returns you to where you were in the list, not to the top.

Version 1.20 13 November 2026

  • Series numbers on covers. Inside a series, each cover carries its number in the series, so the reading order is clear at a glance. A book whose number is not known has no number and is listed after the rest.
  • A series is always shown in reading order, so the Sort button no longer appears there. The switch between grid and list stays.

Version 1.19

  • A tidier Series list. The Series list shows series with two or more books. A series with a single book is left off to keep the list short.
  • Typing in the search box still finds every series, single books included, and a series joins the list by itself once a second book arrives.

Version 1.18

  • Steadier screens, tighter spacing. Every book tile keeps room for a two-line title and an author, so rows on Home and in the Library are one height and nothing below them shifts as you scroll.
  • The gaps between filter buttons are halved, sideways and between rows, throughout the app.
  • Search boxes are slimmer, leaving more of the screen for books.

Version 1.17

  • Clearer sorting progress. Sorting progress is shown as two plain counts, books to look up and books to double-check, on the notification, on Home and on the Categories screen, so the three always agree.

Version 1.16

  • Library filters. A row of filters for what a book is: Fiction, Non-Fiction and Children's & Young Adult, each with its book count, followed by the five genres holding the most books.
  • A second row for where a book is and how far you have read: On phone, In Drive, Unread, Reading, Finished, To read, Favourites and Recently added.
  • Filters from different groups work together, so Fiction, Unread and On phone shows the unread fiction that is on the phone.
  • A filter that would find nothing is left out, and your choice is kept for as long as the app is open.

Version 1.15

  • Series names from a catalogue. Optional series look-up through the Hardcover catalogue. With a Hardcover token in Settings, a book's series name and number are replaced when the author matches and the title is all but identical.
  • Different spellings of the same series are merged into one.
  • A series you set yourself under Edit details is never changed.

Version 1.14

  • Fiction, Non-Fiction and Children's. Categories open on three groups, as a bookshop does: Fiction, Non-Fiction, and Children's & Young Adult.
  • A Check again button on the Unsorted screen sends every unsorted book round the catalogues once more.
  • Headings use British names, such as Humour and Politics.
  • Unsorted books are first in line for a second opinion.

Version 1.13

  • Sorting for very large libraries. A status line shows what the sorter is doing.
  • Sorting could stall on a library of many thousands of books. Catalogue requests are now paced, and Shelfmark waits when a catalogue asks it to slow down, then carries on.

Version 1.12

  • Scans that carry on. Adding or removing a folder part-way through restarts the read with the new list.
  • Reading your Drive folders stopped if you left the settings screen. It now carries on while you browse or read, and so does the scan of the phone.

Version 1.11

  • Drive books as they are found. Books in your Drive folders appear in the Library as they are found, not all together at the end.
  • A dropped connection during a long Drive read is retried, where before it ended the read with an error.

Version 1.10

  • Choose your Drive folders. A folder picker for the Drive library. Shelfmark reads the folders you choose and nothing outside them.

Version 1.9

  • Duplicates. Opening Duplicates could close the app when the library held the same book in two formats.

Version 1.8

  • Voice sample. The sentence used to try out a read-aloud voice is reworded so that voices pronounce it correctly.

Version 1.7

  • Voice names. A short explanation of how read aloud works, on the voice settings screen.
  • The list of read-aloud voices shows plain names in place of technical codes.

Version 1.6

  • Page turns, themes and reading aids. Curl and Fade page turns, alongside Slide.
  • Five more colour themes, and one you can make yourself.
  • Auto-scroll, at a speed you set.
  • A reading ruler and a warm filter for night reading, both off until you turn them on.
  • A choice of tap layouts, and page turning from a Bluetooth remote or headset buttons.
  • A quiet daily reading goal on Home, with no reminders.
  • The text sheet inside a book keeps to the basics, with everything else under one More options row.

Version 1.5

  • Tap to turn. With the Slide page turn, a tap could turn the page and then slide straight back. Taps and swipes are now told apart reliably.

Version 1.4

  • Classics, fonts and PDF options. Four public-domain classics come with the app: Pride and Prejudice, A Study in Scarlet, The Picture of Dorian Gray and The Time Machine. Delete any you do not want and they stay deleted.
  • Four more reading fonts: EB Garamond, Merriweather, Lora and Libre Baskerville.
  • Two PDF switches in Settings, for showing PDFs in the Library and for searching inside them.

Version 1.3

  • Page numbers. The page number goes up by one with every page turn and reads, for example, Page 13 of about 1,270. A switch in Settings brings back fixed-size pages.

Version 1.2

  • Opening books. If a first page has not appeared after 20 seconds, Shelfmark says so.
  • Error screens have a Copy details button, to make a problem easy to report.
  • On some phones a book could sit on Opening and never show its first page. The reader's own pages are now always allowed to load, while outside web addresses stay blocked.

Version 1.1

  • Better sorting. A Documents heading for PDFs and text files that no catalogue recognises as books.
  • An optional Google Books key in Settings, used to sharpen sub-headings.
  • The sorting rules cover many more subjects, so more books find a proper heading.
  • Apple's book catalogue is asked for a second opinion when Open Library has little to say about a book.
  • Books left in Unsorted are sorted again the first time this version opens.

Version 1.0

  • Opens EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, TXT, RTF, FB2, and CBZ and CBR comics.
  • Finds the books on your phone and, if you sign in, in your Google Drive.
  • Looks each book up by title and author and files it under a standard bookshop heading.
  • Paged reading with tap zones, a choice of fonts and text settings, and day and night colours that follow the phone.
  • Highlights, notes and bookmarks, gathered from every book on one screen.
  • Read aloud with the voices on your phone.
  • Reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes and collections kept in step between devices through your own Google Drive.
  • Series and author shelves, collections, a duplicate finder and a home-screen widget.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.22 is called Shelfmark.apk. It is 13.0 MB (13,049,534 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

4945be9df1b9581745f00376288a9edc1cc5a903b0405c38fb3967174abfd1be

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Shelfmark.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
