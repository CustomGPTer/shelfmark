Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026. This policy covers the Shelfmark app for Android (package uk.jj.shelfmark) and this website. Shelfmark is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • Shelfmark has no accounts and no servers of its own. The developer never receives your books, your reading data or anything from your Google account.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Your library and reading data are stored on your phone. If you turn on sync, a copy of your reading data is stored in your own Google Drive.

What stays on your phone

The list of your books, their covers and categories, your reading position, bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections, settings and a record of your reading (how long you read each book, and when) are kept in the app's private storage on your device.

To find ebooks, Shelfmark asks for access to the files on your phone. It looks for ebook files only, and nothing it finds is sent anywhere except as described below.

Google user data

Signing in to Google is optional and is off until you turn it on. Shelfmark then asks for up to two permissions, depending on what you switch on.

How it is used

Only to provide the features above. Shelfmark reads the folders you choose and nothing else in your Drive. It never changes, moves, shares or deletes your Drive files.

Where it is stored

On your phone, in the app's private storage. Downloaded books are saved on your phone. The sync file is stored in your own Google Drive.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Google user data is not sent to the developer, not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train any AI or machine-learning model, and not seen by any person other than you.

The one use outside your phone and your Drive is the catalogue lookup described in the next section. It sends a book's title and author, taken from its file name or from the book itself, so the book can be sorted into a category.

Shelfmark's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your phone is kept until you remove it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Shelfmark deletes all of it.
  • The sync file stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Shelfmark and choose Delete hidden app data.
  • You can withdraw Shelfmark's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by turning the switches off in the app.

Your rights

UK and EU data protection law gives you rights over personal data that an organisation holds about you, including the right to see it, correct it and have it deleted.

The developer of Shelfmark holds no personal data about you: there is no account, no server and no log of who uses the app. Your data is on your own phone and in your own Google account, where you control it directly using the steps above. If you need help doing that, use the contact link below. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

Every connection the app makes uses HTTPS. The app's data is held in private storage that other apps cannot read, and is left out of Android's automatic backups.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub, as the host, receives the internet address of visitors and handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app file is downloaded from GitHub as well, on the same terms.

Children

Shelfmark is a general-purpose reading app. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact

For a question about this policy or about your data, open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please do not include personal details in one.
