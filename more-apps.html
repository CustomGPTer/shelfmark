More apps

Shelfmark has siblings. Each is an Android app made by the same developer, free, with no adverts and nothing to unlock. Each button downloads the latest app file straight from that app's own page on GitHub.

  • Grab

    A video downloader without the adverts

    Grab has its own ad-blocking browser. Open a page with a video on it, tap the round Download button, choose a size, and the file is saved to your phone.

    Download GrabGrab website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later, 64-bit.

  • PDF+

    A PDF reader, editor and scanner

    Read, annotate, fill and sign, edit, scan, convert, compress and redact. PDF+ brings the tools other PDF apps charge for into one free app.

    Download PDF+PDF+ website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 11 or later.

  • Clearcut

    A background remover that works on your phone

    One tap cuts the subject out of a photo, and hand tools tidy the rest. Save it see-through, put something new behind it, or make it a WhatsApp sticker. Your photos are never uploaded.

    Download ClearcutClearcut website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later, 64-bit.

  • Vigil

    A spare phone as a security camera

    Vigil turns a phone you no longer use into a camera for your home, your pets or your site cabin. It spots people, vehicles and sounds, and saves clips to your own Google Drive.

    Download VigilVigil website

    Free, with no adverts. Android 10 or later.

Installing one

These apps install the same way Shelfmark does. Chrome may say the file might be harmful. It shows this for app files that do not come from Google Play. Choose Download anyway. Open the downloaded file, allow your browser to install apps if Android asks, and if Google Play Protect steps in, choose More details, then Install anyway.

Each app has its own website with its release notes, privacy policy and terms, and its own place to report a problem.
